Crown Rescue – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Crown Rescue ("the game") is a single-player puzzle game for Android.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal or device information. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs, and it does not request internet access.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, boosters and sound settings) is saved locally on your device only. It never leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or choose Settings → Reset Progress.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because no data is collected, no personal information from children is collected either.

Permissions

The game may use vibration for haptic feedback, which can be turned off in Settings. No other permissions are used.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer e-mail shown on the game's Google Play page.
